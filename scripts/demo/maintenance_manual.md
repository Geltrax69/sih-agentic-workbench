# P-Series Pump Maintenance Manual (excerpt)

## Over-temperature shutdowns
Over-temperature shutdowns on P-series pumps are typically caused by:
1. Blocked cooling fins (dust accumulation in dusty environments)
2. Bearing wear increasing friction load
3. Low coolant level in closed-loop systems

## Procedure: Bearing replacement (P-17 class)
1. Depressurize and lock out the pump.
2. Remove coupling guard and coupling.
3. Extract bearing housing; inspect shaft for scoring.
4. Fit new bearing (part BRG-4210), torque to 45 Nm.
5. Reassemble, run at 50% load for 10 minutes, check temperature.

## Escalation
If over-temperature shutdowns recur within 30 days of bearing replacement,
escalate to the reliability engineer and check the coolant loop.
